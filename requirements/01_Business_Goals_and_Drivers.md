# Business Goals & Drivers
This document covers the whole estate: Visitor Journey and Growth, Crowd and Site Operations, Ride Operations and Asset Reliability, and Animal Care and Welfare. 

The core functionality is to sell and validate access, provide visitor information, manage rides and animal care, coordinate site operations, and report on estate performance. These functions support the business goals below.

## Business Drivers

| Business Driver | Primary Area | Supporting Areas | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 — Financial Viability | Estate Management | Visitors, Animals, Rides, Site Operations | Sustained financial losses threaten the estate’s future and require improved revenue, cost control and investment discipline. |
| D2 — Investment Prioritisation | Estate Management | Visitors, Animals, Rides, Site Operations | Limited evidence about attraction demand, condition and operating costs makes it difficult to direct investment where it will deliver the greatest benefit. |
| D3 — Sustainable Attendance Growth | Visitors | Estate Management, Site Operations | The estate needs a larger visitor base to support its financial future, while ensuring attractions and operations can accommodate increased demand. |
| D4 — Visitor Value and Loyalty | Visitors | Rides, Animals, Site Operations | The estate needs to understand what visitors value and address barriers to enjoyable visits, returning and recommending the estate to others. |
| D5 — Effective Daily Operations | Site Operations | Visitors, Animals, Rides | Staff need timely information about queues, attraction availability and incidents to deploy resources effectively and respond to changing conditions. |
| D6 — Ageing Ride Sustainability | Rides | Site Operations, Estate Management | Historic rides require ongoing maintenance to preserve their safe operation, reduce disruption and make effective use of engineering resources. |
| D7 — Management Visibility and Accountability | Estate Management | Visitors, Animals, Rides, Site Operations | Estate leadership needs a consistent view of performance to assess progress, identify underperformance and hold improvement initiatives accountable. |
| D8 — Animal Welfare Assurance | Animals | Site Operations | The estate must understand and respond to animal health and feeding concerns, and concerning changes in the jumping piranha population, to fulfil its care responsibilities. |

## Business Goals

Business outcomes describe benefits to the estate and its visitors. Supporting management and operational objectives describe the performance needed to achieve those benefits. Delivery milestones are listed separately.

“Year 1” means the first 12 months after the relevant capability enters operational use. Baselines are established during the first eight weeks using comparable historical and seasonal data where available.

| Business Goal | Goal Type | Business Strategy | Measure |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 — Reach an operating break-even position by the end of year 3. | Business Outcome | Grow revenue, control operating costs and prioritise investments by financial return. | Operating result against a consistent accounting boundary. |
| G2 — Grow average daily attendance from approximately 5,000 to at least 15,000 within three years while maintaining visitor experience, welfare and safe operating capacity. | Business Outcome | Make ticket and family-pass selection, purchasing and payment easy and reliable; attract new visitors and expand operational capacity. | Average daily attendance; purchase-task completion, completion time and visitor feedback as supporting measures, alongside welfare and safe operating capacity. |
| G3 — Increase the proportion of visitors returning within 12 months by 10 percentage points by the end of year 3. | Business Outcome | Use visitor feedback, relevant offers and targeted engagement to encourage return visits. | The proportion returning within 12 months, using comparable visitor cohorts. |
| G4 — During year 1, reduce queue-related dissatisfaction and unexpected-attraction-unavailability dissatisfaction by 20% each relative to their survey baselines. | Business Outcome | Provide current attraction and queue information; coordinate staffing and maintenance. | Two separate survey rates: excessive-queue dissatisfaction and unexpected-unavailability dissatisfaction. |
| G5 — Reduce unplanned ride downtime by 30% relative to baseline during year 1, while meeting safety obligations and the agreed maintenance budget. | Business Outcome | Use proactive inspections, condition monitoring and targeted maintenance to prevent recurring failures. | Unplanned unavailable time as a proportion of scheduled operating time, alongside maintenance spend and compliance with required inspections and work. |
| G6 — Within 12 months, ensure every major estate investment has an evidence-based business case, a benefit owner and a scheduled results review; assign material departures from business targets an owner and corrective action by the next monthly review. | Management Objective | Use performance evidence to govern investment and follow through on underperformance. | Coverage of business cases, benefit ownership and results reviews; timely assignment of corrective actions and subsequent closure evidence. |
| G7 — Within six months, detect concerning piranha population changes within a keeper-defined interval and ensure every confirmed concern receives an appropriate response within its care deadline. | Operational Objective | Validate population trends and investigate concerns with keeper oversight. | Detection delay and timely response to confirmed concerns, supported by validated estimates and documented uncertainty. |
| G8 — By the end of year 1, reduce overdue routine feeding and welfare activities against baseline, with no critical care deadline missed. | Operational Objective | Prioritise timely care and escalate missed activities and welfare concerns. | Overdue routine-care rate, critical missed deadlines and response times to welfare concerns; recording completeness is supporting evidence. |
| G9 — By the end of year 1, reduce incident-to-action time and overdue routine site tasks against baseline, measured separately by priority. | Operational Objective | Coordinate daily staffing and respond promptly to changing site conditions. | Median and 95th-percentile incident-to-action time, plus the proportion of overdue routine site tasks, segmented by priority. |

### Shared Delivery Conditions

- **Continuity:** Support agreed essential workflows through patchy connectivity, with local operation where appropriate, buffered observations and controlled reconciliation.
- **Authority and safety:** Qualified people retain responsibility for animal-care decisions and ride return to service; certified ride controls remain independent of analytics and AI. Efficiency targets must not incentivise bypassing care or safety obligations.
- **Data ownership:** Each capability owns its operational records and publishes the information others need. Shared reporting combines those records without creating conflicting sources of truth.
- **Scope Boundaries:** General animal-population management, virtual queuing, new paid events and specific pass media are not prerequisites for the goals above.
