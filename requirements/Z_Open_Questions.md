# Open Questions

## Business Goals and Delivery Assumptions

The attendance ambition in G2 comes from the brief. The remaining numerical goals are architecture-team targets that require validation; they are not measured results or confirmed estate commitments.

| Reference | Open Question | Suggested Owner |
|---|---|---|
| G1 | What accounting boundary defines operating break-even, how are modernisation costs treated, and is break-even within three years financially achievable? | Estate Management / Finance |
| G1–G9 | What date starts the six-month and three-year periods, and how do phased capability launches align with the estate-wide timeline? | Estate Management / Delivery Lead |
| G2 | Which representative ticket and family-pass journeys will measure purchase-task completion and time, and what acceptance thresholds and feedback method will demonstrate ease of purchasing? | Ticketing / Visitor Services |
| G3 | What is the current 12-month return rate, how will repeat visitors be identified appropriately, and is a 10-percentage-point improvement achievable? | Visitor Services / Privacy Lead |
| G4 | What separate survey questions and comparable sampling will measure queue and unexpected-unavailability dissatisfaction, and does each baseline support a 20% reduction? | Visitor Services / Site Operations |
| G5 | What downtime baseline and maintenance budget apply, and is a 30% reduction achievable without deferring required inspections or work? | Ride Engineering |
| G6 | What defines a major investment or material departure from target, who owns benefits and corrective actions, and what constitutes acceptable review evidence? | Estate Management / Finance |
| G7 | What population changes are concerning, what uncertainty is acceptable, how will detection delay be validated, and what monitoring interval and response deadlines will keepers approve? | Animal-Care Lead |
| G8 | What defines overdue routine care and critical care deadlines by species, what reduction target is achievable, and how will completed care be verified independently of record completeness? | Animal-Care Lead |
| G9 | What starts the incident-response clock, what counts as action, how are priorities and task deadlines defined, and what reduction targets are achievable? | Site Operations |
| Reporting Milestone | Can all 40 rides and 55 displays/enclosures provide reliable data within six months, and who owns report production and quality? | Data Lead / Domain Owners |
| Delivery Milestones | How will capability rollout dates align with outcome measurement periods so benefits can be evaluated after adoption? | Delivery Lead / Domain Owners |
| All Goals | What historical and seasonal data is available, and what validation period is needed if the first eight weeks cannot establish representative baselines? | Domain Owners / Data Lead |
| Shared Continuity | Which workflows must continue offline, for how long, and what reconciliation or admission-risk limits apply? | Platform Lead / Domain Owners |
| Scope Extensions | Should general population management, virtual queuing, paid events or specific pass media enter scope, or remain deferred options? | Estate Management / Product Lead |
