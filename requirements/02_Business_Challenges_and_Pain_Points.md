# Business Challenges & Pain Points

## Business Challenges

- Reverse sustained financial losses and make the estate profitable.
- Grow average daily attendance from approximately 5,000 to 15,000 within three years while maintaining visitor experience and operational effectiveness.
- Maintain 40 historic rides and care for a varied animal collection across 55 displays and enclosures.
- Direct investment and staffing using evidence of attraction popularity, operating performance and care needs.
- Encourage repeat visits and recommendations by understanding what visitors enjoy.
- Modernise operations across a large estate with patchy connectivity.

## Pain Points of the Business

- Limited knowledge of which rides, exhibits and areas are popular makes investment and staffing decisions difficult.
- Queue locations and visitor movement need better visibility to help visitors navigate the estate.
- Ageing rides require regular maintenance; the estate wants a more proactive approach.
- Management needs to identify where maintenance problems affect popular rides.
- Animal care is costly, with sickness adding further expense and welfare concerns.
- Staff need better information about animal health, feeding and food consumption.
- Changes in the jumping piranha population need monitoring so concerning trends can be investigated.
- The estate lacks a clear understanding of how to encourage visitors to return and bring others.
- Easy ticket purchasing, including family passes, is an explicit need; the current purchasing process is not described.
- Patchy Wi-Fi complicates collecting and sharing operational information across the estate and with cloud services.
