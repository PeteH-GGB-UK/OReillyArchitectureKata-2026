Architecture Kata Intro – Judging Rubric Summary

# Key Principle

The kata is primarily about demonstrating architectural thinking, communication, reasoning and trade-offs rather than producing the perfect architecture.

# What Sam Said Judges Want

- Clear, concise documentation rather than AI-generated volume.

- Evidence of decision making and trade-offs.

- Architectural Decision Records (ADRs) explaining why decisions were made.

- Appropriate and justified use of AI.

- Plans for handling AI uncertainty and vendor change.

- AI validation, testing, and monitoring approaches.

- Good communication and diagrams.

- Strong linkage to business outcomes.

# Scoring Areas

- Clarity of thought

- Why decisions were made, not just what was chosen

- Quality of ADRs

- Use of AI where it creates value

- Flexibility to cope with changing AI models/providers

- Validation and verification of AI outputs

- Appropriate level of detail

- Communication quality

- Business outcome focus

# Judge Focuses

- Gayathri Mohan: clarity of thought, trade-offs, business outcome prioritization.

- Ben Stopford: AI value, evolvability, event-driven thinking, validation and enforceable ADRs.

# AJG Team Recommendation

- 2–3-page executive narrative

- High level C4 context diagram

- Architecture diagram covering IoT, MQTT, data platform and AI

- 5-10 strong ADRs

- AI governance section

- Phased roadmap
