## Background

Von Digitalis Estates is a large historic estate whose traditional business has failed. The new owner must make the estate profitable by modernising a historic amusement park of around 40 rides and an exotic animal collection of more than 200 animals across 55 enclosures. The estate currently attracts about 5,000 visitors per day and needs to reach 15,000 within three years, while improving repeat visits, understanding attraction demand, and controlling the cost of animal care.

This architecture kata explores how digital services, IoT data, cloud platforms, and practical AI can support that transformation. The solution must work across an estate with patchy Wi-Fi, move data reliably to the cloud, improve both visitor and operator experiences, and use AI responsibly for insights such as personalisation, demand forecasting, predictive maintenance, and animal-health monitoring. The architecture should make those capabilities measurable, resilient, and adaptable as AI technologies evolve.
